# Having only 2TB of storage forces you to think about backups and organize.

My Arch Linux workstation and MacBook only have 2TB drives, which would be plenty for normal usage.

However, as a video content creator, I frequently generate video files that take up space.

Having only 2TB to work with forces me to have a strategy for organization and backups.

I'm currently in the process of implementing a full 3,2,1 backup strategy for all of my files and media.
